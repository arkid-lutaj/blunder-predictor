======================================================================
CAVEAT, and why it does not sink the result
======================================================================
  Puzzles are SELECTED so a blunder exists and the best move is
  unique. They are therefore a biased sample of positions, and this
  is not a claim about all chess positions.

  But the bias runs in a useful direction. Conditioning on 'a mistake
  is on offer here' is close to the regime the Phase 7 SELECTION term
  describes, and Phase 7 found selection carries ~100% of the rating
  effect while availability carries none. So this correlation is
  evidence about the selection channel specifically, which is exactly
  the channel that mattered.

  It is still external: the puzzle rating comes from human solve
  attempts on lichess.org, not from this dump, this label or these
  features.
