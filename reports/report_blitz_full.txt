30.64% of positives sit within 5 win% of the cut, and 128,474 negatives sit
just under it. Depth-dependent eval jitter in the Lichess annotations moves rows
across the line in BOTH directions, so the irreducible label noise is roughly
this band. It caps how well ANY model can score and attenuates measured
effects; it does not by itself make a smaller gain fake. The test that
matters is whether model ranking is stable at thresholds 15, 20 and 25.

==============================================================================
3c. STRUCTURAL FLOOR (label cannot fire in lost positions)
==============================================================================
cp clamped to +/-1000 => win% is clamped to [2.46, 97.54]
so a t-point drop needs winpct_before > 2.46 + t

POV matters here: cp_before is WHITE-POV as Lichess writes it, while
winpct_before and win_drop are MOVER-POV. The floor is a statement about
the mover, so the cp cut-offs below are mover-POV too (negate them to
read them off cp_before for a Black move).

  winpct_before source: winpct_before column (mover-POV)
  max |stored - recomputed from cp_before| win%: 0.0000 (OK)
threshold  impossible when  impossible rows   share  positives there  eligible base rate
---------  ---------------  ---------------  ------  ---------------  ------------------
15         mover cp < -422          591,370   9.44%                0              6.628%
20         mover cp < -337          779,300  12.44%                0              4.513%
25         mover cp < -264          940,211  15.01%                0              3.225%

`positives there` is (win_drop > t) counted inside the region where a
t-point drop is arithmetically impossible, so it MUST be 0 on every row.
A non-zero count is a parser inconsistency, not a chess fact.

Two consequences worth carrying into the README:
  1. The label is blind to blunders in already-lost positions. Dropping a
     rook while down a queen does not register. That is a deliberate
     consequence of using win probability rather than centipawn loss.
  2. engine_assisted gets winpct_before, so part of its advantage is just
     learning 'below the floor, predict zero'. Always report that
     comparison ALSO on the eligible subset, where the free win vanishes.
