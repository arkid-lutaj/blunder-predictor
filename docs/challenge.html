Blunder Challenge

Real positions from Lichess blitz games. Play the move you would actually play, and find out what it cost.
What is going on here
  1. Every legal move in these positions was scored by Stockfish in advance, so the page knows what any move you play is worth. There is no engine running in your browser.
  2. Separately, a model was trained to predict how often humans of a given rating blunder in a position. It never saw the answers.
  3. Because the model knows how hard each position is, your hit rate on a handful of them is enough to estimate your rating.
