## Data

**Lichess open database** - <https://database.lichess.org>
Source of every position used for training and evaluation. The June 2026
standard rated dump was parsed locally; the dump itself is not redistributed
here (it is 28 GB and gitignored).

`data/sample_games.pgn` contains **500 games copied verbatim** from that dump
so the pipeline and the CI can run without a download. Each game keeps its
original `[Site "https://lichess.org/..."]` header, so individual games remain
traceable to their source.

**Lichess puzzle database** - <https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles>
Used for the external validation in `src/validate_puzzles.py`. Not
redistributed; the CSV is gitignored.

Lichess publishes both databases in the public domain under the Creative
Commons CC0 licence. Player usernames appear in the sample PGN because they are
part of the published records.

## Method

**The win-percentage formula.** The label is built on

    win% = 50 + 50 * (2 / (1 + exp(-0.00368208 * cp)) - 1)

The constant `0.00368208` and this form are **Lichess's**, documented at
<https://lichess.org/page/accuracy>. It is not derived here and is not original
to this project. Defined once per script as `WIN_K`.

The 20-point drop threshold, the clamping of `cp` to +/-1000, the
availability/selection decomposition, the D10 difficulty measure and the
information-ranking of demo positions are this project's own choices.

**Glicko2** is the rating system Lichess uses. Ratings are consumed as given;
nothing here implements or modifies it.

## Removed: piece artwork

An earlier version of the interactive demo embedded the twelve piece graphics
from `chess.svg.PIECES` in **python-chess**, copied verbatim into
`docs/challenge.json`.

That was a licence conflict and it has been removed. python-chess is
**GPL-3.0+** (confirmed from its package metadata and the GPL v3 text bundled
in its distribution), so publishing its assets inside an MIT-licensed file was
not permissible. The installed package also carries no separate notice for the
artwork itself, which made the art's own provenance impossible to attribute
accurately from the source it was taken from.

The board now draws the **Unicode chess characters** U+265A to U+265F. Those
are codepoints in the Unicode standard rather than anyone's artwork, so there
is nothing to attribute and nothing to infringe. No third-party image, icon,
font or SVG is bundled anywhere in this repository.
